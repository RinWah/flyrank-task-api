i love coffee

i love coffee, but it makes my anxiety and heart palpitations severe.

but i also love matcha

i found my love for matcha after quitting coffee on a daily basis. it doesn't spike my heartrate.

but matcha > coffee for me

matcha has less side effects but still is a great way for me to start my day.

i think any caffeinated drink is fine as long as in moderation. no judgement here!
